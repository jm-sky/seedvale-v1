# Q09 and G06 — Trade Quests Curation

**Created at:** 2026-10-14  
**Created by:** ChatGPT Sol

**Status:** Accepted narrative direction (D-QUEST-31); exact casting, placement, economy tuning and implementation deferred.

## Accepted decision

- **Keep/rewrite both**, without merging: V2 Q09 `Goods on the Ground` concerns negotiation and real movement of tools and household goods; V2 G06 `Trader's Letter` concerns a grain-price proposal and its social effects. They are distinct incidents with Stephen Chapman as a common character.
- **Stephen's motivation:** he is honest in direct bargains but cautious and economically self-interested. He sincerely believes keeping grain prices high can protect winter reserves, while knowing he stands to profit. Present this as a coherent, morally mixed personality, not an abrupt conversion into a dishonest merchant.
- **Placement is flexible:** these quests are **not required to occur in the home settlement**. They may take place there if the canonical NPC cast, households, routes and economic circumstances fit. During M7 casting, choose a plausible settlement or small settlement pair based on established world geography, rather than duplicating existing traders or moving reserved identities. Keep Stephen distinct from Kate Hornblower (Q-012).
- Q09 can establish optional background knowledge for G06. Prefer Q09 occurring earlier, but **do not gate G06 on completion of Q09**; its own lead, characters and information must work independently.
- Preserve Q09's three broad approaches: direct barter, consignment with later settlement, or order for a future trip. Real goods, physical transport constraints, inventories, ownership and actual payments matter more than quest flags.
- Preserve G06's four broad choices: support coordinated high prices, negotiate a cap/reserve, refuse the arrangement, or bring it to a public decision. Effects must reflect the material burden of high prices on poorer households, with persistent, locally knowable social consequences.
- Do not require generic NPC work-speed modifiers, market-wide dynamic inventory optimisation or a complete market simulator to ship either quest. An authored, bounded seasonal economic effect may later integrate with M8 economy.
- Ordinary rewards and transfers should come from actual world resources; local reputation and individual relationships remain distinct. Refusal and non-participation must not freeze the world.

## Reconciliation and boundaries

- V1 `zaginiona-przesylka` has a separate accepted rewrite (D-QUEST-20); V1 `zaginiony-ladunek` is a separate storm-shipwreck story (D-QUEST-26). Neither is merged with Q09/G06.
- Names, households, fixed settlement tags `H`/`V`/`T`, absolute prices, reward amounts, price multipliers, timing, implementation stubs and legacy quest code remain **reference**, not settled SeedVale canon.
- Use the existing future quest, NPC, item, inventory, trade, knowledge, dialogue and economic systems. Avoid a parallel quest-only trade or price engine.
- Implementation stays gated by M7, with economy integration aligned to M8. No gameplay code change is authorized by this decision.
