## Summary

Piotr lacks stone to strengthen a foundation in the settlement. The player gathers six stones near rocks and delivers them. If the foundation was already fixed by the player, the quest resolves as `already_repaired`.

## Stages / objectives

- Gather 6 `stone` items (`gather_item`, count 6).

## Outcomes & rewards

- `delivered` (complete, reward shown): 9 coins and relation +1 with Piotr.
- `already_repaired` (complete): no reward.
