## Accepted current direction (D-QUEST-23)

- **Keep** V1 `lis-przy-osadzie` as a **simple quest: kill the fox** threatening poultry near the home settlement. Mark Hornblower, the home guard, is the quest giver.
- **Retire** V2 Q01 `A Hare Out of Place` from the curated quest inventory. Do **not** transfer Luke's apprentice storyline into this quest.
- **No young apprentice, Jacob training storyline, second-fox reveal, or elaborate multi-route narrative** in this quest. The previous two-fox and trap-centered branching proposals are superseded by the author's explicit request for a simple single-fox kill quest.
- The fox's death must correspond to an actual animal/world-state outcome, not merely a quest-only flag.
- **`fox_pelt` (Fox Pelt)** remains an accepted new inventory item: a fox skin with fur, obtainable by skinning a killed fox; it may be traded or used in crafting. The precise item statistics, pelt quality, prices and crafting recipient remain open. This item is **not an extra mandatory quest objective**.
- The legacy V1 reward (20 coins, competence/courage +3, renown +3) is reference only; balance has not been re-approved.
- **Separate future direction:** a palisade-building quest after several other quests. Its prerequisites and scope remain undefined and should not be imposed on the fox quest.

## Superseded proposals (historical, not requirements)

The earlier curation considered Luke proving himself, Jacob's dislike of traps, two guaranteed foxes, lethal and live-capture traps with different pelt condition, and an optional crafter seeking fox fur. These do **not** define the current authored fox quest. General trap mechanics, if pursued independently, require their own design decision.
