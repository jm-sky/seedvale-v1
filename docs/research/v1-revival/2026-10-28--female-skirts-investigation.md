## 1. Executive summary

- `EQ_PeasantSkirt` is one procedurally lofted cone. It is 224 vertices / 392 triangles, 28 segments × 6 rows, with its hem at mid-calf (z 0.42 m). It is generated by `scripts/assets/blender-dress-modules.py` and attached at runtime to **every female NPC**: all adult outfits are coerced to Peasant, Peasant_Boots, Herbalist or Wizard, and female children wear Peasant. **inferred** (code)
- **The knee and calf clipping is mainly a skinning defect, not a geometry or runtime defect.** At rest the skirt clears the Peasant legs by ≥ 10 cm at knee and hem height. The weights, however, keep 41 % pelvis at knee height, and the left/right thigh blend averages the front and back centre panels 50/50. The panel in front of the stepping knee therefore follows only ~29–43 % of the thigh. **measured**
- Two secondary defects come from geometry and fit:
  - **Peasant_Boots:** the Ranger boot cuffs (top at z 0.56 m) sit inside the hem band, and the rear cuff cuts through the back hem in Walk. **measured + observed**
  - **Wizard (trader, incl. Kasia/Kate Hornblower):** the coat's flaps, trim and belt ornament poke through the skirt **at rest**, by up to 2.3 cm. The skirt's waist ring was fitted to the Peasant bodice, not the Wizard coat. **measured + observed**
- Runtime integration (bone-name rebinding with the eq pack's own inverse bind matrices) reproduces correctly in Blender. Nothing points to a runtime cause. **inferred** (code + Blender reproduction)
- **Prototype result:** a re-weighted skirt removes all measured Walk poke-through and cuts Run (Sprint) poke-through by ~94 %. This uses a front/back-asymmetric thigh share, a front-and-back calf share below the knee, and a slightly fuller hem (0.36 m). The weighting approach also carries over to a LongSkirt prototype. **measured**
- **Recommended approach:** fix the weights and shape in the existing Blender loft script, and reuse the existing eq garment slot. No new clothing system, no runtime cloth, no skeleton change. Seated, kneeling and crouching poses cannot be made clean on the 65-bone skeleton with linear blend skinning alone. They are recorded as a known limitation for later.

## 3. Method

- Temporary Blender scene: decoded (meshopt removed) copies of `Female_Peasant.glb`, `Female_Peasant_Boots.glb`, `Female_Wizard.glb`, `eq/Female.glb` and `anims.glb` from `public/assets/characters/`. Module skeletons were re-based onto the body skeleton by the translation measured above, and the V1 clips were assigned to the body armature. Production files were not modified.
- **Poke-through metric:** for each sampled frame, a body (legs + feet) vertex counts as poking through when
  - it lies on the outer side of the nearest skirt face (along that face's normal), and
  - a horizontal ray from it towards the pelvis axis crosses the skirt.

  The ray test excludes legs that are simply visible below the hem. Reported per clip: **area** = Σ over samples of poking vertices deeper than 1 cm; **max** depth (search radius 20 cm, so 20.0 = saturated); **frames** = samples with any poke. 17 samples per clip.
- Limits: the metric also flags a skirt panel that has flipped over a thigh (seated poses), so a non-zero seated value can be a "floating tray" rather than a leg showing. Every important case was confirmed visually. No heads, hair or arms were included in the clipping test.

### 4.3 Limitations found while prototyping (deep hip flexion)

A heavier thigh share in front removes most seated poke-through (P1 sitting 1377 → 68), but the front panel then becomes a stiff horizontal tray, and the sides flare like a cape. In `Fixing_Kneeling`, every closed-tube variant either lets the raised knee through or balloons into a sack. Cause: linear blend skinning can only average the bones; it cannot follow the forward-most leg, and the UBC skeleton has no skirt bones. Real fixes need one of these:
- corrective morph targets driven by thigh pitch (a new runtime driver), or
- extra skirt bones baked per clip (a skeleton and pipeline change).

Both are outside this task. **observed + inferred**

## 6. Recommended corrective approach and implementation scope (for a later approved task)

1. **Weights (main fix):** add the asymmetric front/back thigh and calf weight function used here to `build_skirt` in `blender-dress-modules.py` (parameters as in §5). Drop the `Fat` morph from skirt exports.
2. **WorkSkirt replaces PeasantSkirt** in the same slot: one garment node, same `baseGarments` rule, no new system. Whether to rename the id (`CharacterGarment`, `eqNodeName`, tests) or keep `EQ_PeasantSkirt` is an author/implementation choice.
3. **LongSkirt:** build it with the same function, add it to `MODULES` (`['raw']`), extend `CharacterGarment`, and choose the roles in `baseGarments`/`npcAppearance`. Kate's named override does not exist yet and is a separate topic.
4. **Wizard fit:** build the skirt waist from `Female_Wizard_Body` for skirted Wizards, or stop combining Wizard with a skirt (see decisions).
5. **Peasant_Boots:** verify the cuff/hem clearance with the final shape (more back flare), or swap to lower boots for skirted women.
6. **Verification for the implementation:**
   - rerun this metric on the rebuilt GLBs (Walk/Run gate: no poke deeper than 1 cm in Walk; Run reduced and visually acceptable);
   - update and check `actorCullBounds.assets.test.ts` (wider hem);
   - review the look in the asset browser and a settlement (browser/manual).
7. Unchanged: runtime code paths, skeleton, save data (appearance is derived, not persisted), determinism and worldgen caches.

Cost estimate (inferred): per female NPC, +112 tris (WorkSkirt) or +280 tris (LongSkirt) over today; draw calls and materials unchanged.

## 7. Open decisions for the author

1. **WorkSkirt as the replacement** for PeasantSkirt (recommended), or a third, separate skirt?
2. **WorkSkirt silhouette:** plain A-line, or kirtled-up (shorter front)? Hem height (0.40 m mid-calf proposed)?
3. **LongSkirt hem:** true ankle (accept a slight heel touch in Walk), or just above the ankle?
4. **Who wears LongSkirt:** traders only? There is no innkeeper/waitress role in `Role` today.
5. **Wizard/trader:** keep the Wizard coat with a skirt (needs a dedicated waist fit), or dress female traders in Peasant top + LongSkirt? This affects Kate Hornblower's "green dress".
6. **Colours:**
   - undyed wool, faded madder, green, woad?
   - Should skirts vary per NPC? Today all skirts share one colour; variety needs baked variants or per-NPC material clones (cost).
7. **Acceptable artefacts:** forward "tent" in Walk and strong shearing in Run, versus residual poke-through.
8. **Later:** is seated/kneeling quality worth corrective morphs (option F)?
