## Summary

Rats are infesting the settlement's storage and eating supplies. Marek asks the player to repair the storage, destroy the nest behind one of the houses, and reduce the number of living rats in the settlement to one or fewer. The quest completes when all three conditions hold.

## Stages / objectives

- Resolve the storage rat infestation (`resolve_storage_rat_infestation`): storage repaired, nest destroyed, living rats at most 1.

## Outcomes & rewards

- `infestation_cleared` (complete): relation +2 with Marek, reputation competence +4 and benevolence +3, renown +5. No item reward.
