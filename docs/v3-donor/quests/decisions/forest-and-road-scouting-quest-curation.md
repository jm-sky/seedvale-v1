## D-QUEST-36 — KEEP / REWRITE two separate scouting quests

Retain and rewrite V1 `zwiadowca` (Scouting the Area, Peter Woodward) and V1 `sprawdz-szlak` (Checking the Rock Cave, Kate Hornblower). These are separate requests with different practical purposes, even if the player can fulfil overlapping evidence objectives on a single journey.

### Peter — forest operations

Peter, a woodcutter, scouts potential logging areas to decide where to work and how to move timber safely. The player may examine one or two nearby grottoes/cave entrances (according to actual world geography), animal dens/habitats, and useful access/haulage conditions. Do not require collecting arbitrary mountain stones or a fixed stag sighting. Locations must exist in the authoritative world; do not create quest-only sites or animals. Findings can influence Peter's ordinary logging decisions if supported by shared NPC/work systems, otherwise stage those effects until available.

### Kate — road safety

Kate, a trader, wants credible information about potential risks to travellers and goods **near the road**, not a general wilderness survey. Relevant tracks, nearby caves and dens can be examined. Accurate reports allow her to warn travellers, inform Mark or judge the route safer using ordinary NPC knowledge and communication. Her objectives and motivation remain separate from Peter's forest interests.

### Evidence, branches and outcomes

- A single actual inspection may satisfy relevant objectives in both quests **when the exact same place/evidence is involved**; each quest tracks its own report, relationship and consequences. Never manufacture two different site incidents solely to prevent overlap or double-count an observation as multiple distinct finds.
- Players may report honestly, omit information, or knowingly lie. NPC belief depends on their own knowledge and plausible later evidence; falsehood is not automatically exposed or punished. Consequences can include risky logging decisions or traveller exposure if the reported information was acted upon, bounded by actual world conditions. Keep local relationships and settlement reputation separate, with knowledge/exposure persisting.
- Exploration and learned facts work before accepting either quest; the player can report prior observations. No magical detection of undiscovered sites, no compulsory combat, and no quest-only scouting, NPC AI, fauna or reputation systems.
- Modest rewards should fit each party's real resources and actual assistance. Specific number of sites, locations, NPC actions, dialogue, reward values and timing are M7 design details, not accepted fixed contracts.

## Integration boundaries

Reuse M3 POI/cave discovery and saved knowledge, M6 fauna/den observations and M4 NPC daily life as available, with M7 authored quests/dialogue. A visit to the cave used in the historic V1 Lost Parcel quest is **not** a dependency: D-QUEST-20 rewrote that quest into an unrelated fraud story. Avoid collision with reserved quest fronts and canonical site IDs. Review 024 R-18 identifies shared cave interaction as an overlap, not grounds for merging narratives.
