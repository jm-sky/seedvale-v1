# Patric Smith — character profile

**Status:** author-approved name and personality direction; editorial reference, not runtime implementation  
**Created at:** 2026-10-29  
**Domain:** authored NPC character, dialogue, quests  
**Identity:** Patric Smith, blacksmith of the home settlement; stable V1 NPC binding pending

## Creative anchor

Patric is a serious, responsible and hardworking village blacksmith with a great heart. His imposing build and stern exterior contrast with the warmth he shows those close to him. He enjoys an occasional joke and has a hearty laugh, especially when the day's work allows him to relax.

## Personality

- Dependable, conscientious and proud of doing honest work well.
- Warm and caring with friends and family, even if he does not always express affection in words.
- Particularly fond of children; he makes time for them and treats them kindly.
- Firm and intimidating toward people who act cruelly, dishonestly or prey on the vulnerable.
- Has a good sense of humor without becoming a constant joker; his sternness is not his entire personality.

**Key contrast:** large, work-worn hands and a formidable presence, but a gentle heart. He is not a villainous or perpetually angry smith.

## Dialogue and visual direction

His voice should be grounded, straightforward and confident, with occasional warm teasing or a booming laugh. His mood shifts credibly with context: affectionate and protective among loved ones, serious at work, and unmistakably stern toward malicious behavior. Avoid theatrical threats and generic gruff-blacksmith clichés.

Visual cues: strong, broad-shouldered working blacksmith, powerful hands, practical forge clothing and signs of real craftsmanship. His face can move from focused severity to genuine warmth, especially around children.

## Quest connections

Patric can be the home-settlement smith involved in Kate's surprise decorated shield for Mark, including a possible genuine discount if persuaded after learning of the parcel fraud. The shield, payment and any additional pauldrons must follow the approved physical item and currency transfer rules. He may also fit blacksmith-related stories such as missing tools when their casting and availability are verified.

These are narrative connections, **not** assertions that Patric is already bound to these quests in V1 or that the approved rewrite is implemented.
