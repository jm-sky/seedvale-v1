# Q39 — Cemetery Requirements

**Date:** 2026-10-31
**Status:** Author direction recorded; no implementation

## Author-approved direction

1. **Placement and access**
   - Existing cemeteries can spawn too close to houses or even in the sea; this must be corrected.
   - Cemeteries should be situated slightly outside settlements.
   - A path should connect each cemetery to its settlement.

2. **Visual and spatial reference**
   - V2 cemeteries are the primary reference for Q39.
   - They are not substantially larger than the current V1 cemeteries but have a clear distinction between individual graves.
   - The number of crypts may depend on settlement size.
   - Small settlements should generally have no crypt.

3. **Grave inscriptions**
   - Grave labels/inscriptions are important for locating quest-related graves.
   - They may also communicate the wealth of the deceased.

**Primary reference:** V2 cemetery design.

No model or layout implementation is authorized by this record.
