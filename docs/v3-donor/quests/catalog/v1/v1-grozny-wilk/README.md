## Summary

A dangerous wolf keeps people from leaving the settlement. Anna asks the player to find and kill it. The quest is offered only once Anna's relation is at least trusted. It is the first step of the wolf chain (followed by `wilcza-jama` and `wilki-pod-osada`).

## Stages / objectives

- Kill the dangerous wolf (`kill_target_animal`, `wolf`, `dangerous: true`).

## Outcomes & rewards

- `reported` (complete, reward shown): a damascus long sword, relation +2 with Anna, reputation competence +10, courage +12, benevolence +4, and renown +15.
