Keep the historical [V2 plans](../quests/plans/quests--001--authored-quest-engine-and-starters.md) and [catalogue](../quests/README.md) as reference. Do not transcribe their former Hewer roof/supper cast, retired hare quest, fixed timers, rewards, or quest-only simulation as current requirements.

The [review 094 matrix](../reviews/2026-10-14--094--v1-v2-quest-curation-independent-final-review.md#complete-reconciliation-matrix--all-61-records) is the tracking register: 39 V1 + 22 V2 records; **3 keep, 48 rewrite, 4 merge, 5 retire, 1 defer (Q08)**. The 51 kept/reworked records are an eventual content backlog, **not** an M7 shipping promise. A merged record contributes to its surviving story and cannot pay separately; a retired record cannot be offered; deferred Q08 needs a later content decision. Generated opportunities and the V1 Mine → Colony arc sit outside the 61-record count and get separate rows when scoped. [Author wishes](../notes/author-wishes-gameplay.md) GW-02/03/05/06 are additional inputs, not silently promoted catalogue entries.

Read the relevant [port ledger](../state/PORT-LEDGER.md#economy-and-quests) row and named V1/V2 legacy source before specifying a port. V1 `QuestManager` provides useful world-event/ownership lessons but is coupled to many systems; V2 `questEngine`/`dsl` provides useful declarative stages and bounded active-quest evaluation but its cast, offers and effects reflect superseded stories. Reuse contracts selectively under the current layers and D-QUEST-16; do not copy either engine wholesale.

## Execution discipline

- Every new quest batch starts with its ledger and legacy-source read, a supplier check, a bounded implementation note, and a reviewed content sheet. The 61-row matrix is updated by disposition/status; catalogue metadata stays identified as imported reference.
- Write accepted architecture decisions to [DECISIONS](../decisions/DECISIONS.md) with model and one-line reason. New author questions are batched in [QUESTIONS](../state/QUESTIONS.md) only for real canon/taste/scope conflicts; reversible names and technical IDs use marked defaults.
- Per-slice checks are targeted during iteration. Full checks, quest E2E and multi-day soak are milestone gates, one heavy run at a time. Review actual look and render cost for on-screen changes. Update the [port ledger](../state/PORT-LEDGER.md) only when runtime capability lands, not when a story is curated.
- Never advertise this plan as implementation of all 61 records or as reopening accepted D-QUEST-20…45. An unsupported later ending remains documented and staged, with no counterfeit world effect.
