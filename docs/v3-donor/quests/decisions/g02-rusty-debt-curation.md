## D-QUEST-41 — KEEP / REWRITE

Keep V2 G02 `Rusty Debt` as a separate commercial dispute, narratively connected to Q04 `The Handle Remembers` (D-QUEST-30). Bernard made a flawed plowshare: his damaged hammer (cracked handle/head) causes an uncomfortable rebound in his hand and contributes to the workmanship error. He sincerely believes his work was sound at first, rather than knowingly cheating the buyer. This is a **tool-specific, temporary cause**, not a chronic hand condition, blanket age-based impairment, NPC quality-degradation simulation, or new smithing physics system.

The buyer has a legitimate complaint; Bernard has a legitimate expectation of payment for work but bears responsibility for the defect. Sophie suspects the workmanship problem and can help identify or mend it. Keep four distinct negotiated outcomes from V2 as narrative baselines: (1) reduced-price settlement with the flawed share retained, (2) repair followed by agreed payment, (3) return without payment, (4) pressure for full payment with damaged trust and appropriate social consequences. The repair and Bernard's acknowledgment of fault provide the constructive path, not a forced single correct ending. Reconcile quantities and fairness in M7/M8.

The plowshare is one persistent owned item, not minted on each offer. Exchanges and payments use real inventory, household funds and existing trade/repair services; do not automatically charge a settlement treasury for a household purchase. Discovery of the flaw comes from meaningful examination/conversation, not invisible proximity dwell. Outcomes affect the involved NPC relationships and local knowledge/reputation when justified. If ignored, the parties may resolve the dispute through bounded authored events without waiting forever for the player.

Q04 and G02 **remain independently startable and completable**. Either can provide a clue or an optional dialogue reference to the other; neither is a prerequisite. Reuse the same damaged-hammer fact and Bernard/Sophie identities, subject to canonical M4 casting outside the home reserved identities. Do not fabricate quest-only crafting, item identity, money or NPC AI subsystems.

## Scope

Story curation only. No code implementation before M7; balance, precise wording, available services and timing are deferred.
