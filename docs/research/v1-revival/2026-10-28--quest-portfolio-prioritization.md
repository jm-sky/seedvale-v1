# SeedVale V1 Revival — Complete Quest Portfolio: Scope, Cost & Priority

**Created:** 2026-10-28  
**Status:** **Advisory — pending author decisions.** No quest rewrite, import, merge, retirement, implementation sequence or reward change is approved.  
**Source of truth:** [61-record reconciliation matrix](./2026-10-27--quest-revival-matrix.md) · [recon synthesis](./2026-10-27--quest-revival-recon.md) · [runtime evidence](./2026-10-27--quest-revival-runtime-evidence.md) · [Strategic Brief](./STRATEGIC-BRIEF.md)  
**Direction:** V1 preservation-first; V3 is a selective narrative donor. **D4 Performance-First integration freeze remains binding.** This document is a prioritization aid, not a plan.

## Executive overview

**61 source records: 39 V1 definitions/builder counterparts and 22 V2-only stories.** All 39 have source-confirmed V1 counterparts with conditional availability; registration does **not** prove end-to-end playability or that every seed offers them. V2 additions are candidate *new content*, not repairs. Historical V3 decisions were **3 keep / 48 rewrite / 4 merge / 5 retire / 1 defer**; they describe donor curation, not V1 marching orders.

**Most attractive ROI:** fix observable contradictions and confusing reports; make existing choices legible; give real physical evidence and rewards stronger character context; add *selected* social investigations and relationships absent from V1. V1 already supports real repair reservations, branching, unique items, cave ownership, den destruction, ecology, relationships and saves. Rebuilding those solely to follow V3 would be wasteful. Conversely, a convincing wreck rescue, guaranteed storm, working bridge or stocked consignment is not an XS dialogue change.

**Portfolio balance:** keep short errands and low-pressure ruins visits alongside property dilemmas, special hunts and long treasure chains. New independent stories generally have **Low/Medium canon disruption**, even when costly; impact here measures what changes *existing V1 continuity*, not how dramatic the new story sounds.

**Reading key:** Each entry shows **Status**, player-facing **Story**, **V3 proposal** (including historical disposition), **Recommended V1 action**, **Development effort**, **Narrative impact**, **Expected ROI**, **Dependencies / risks** and **Why**. Effort is *incremental cost in V1* (XS text/review; S bounded content; M connected interactions; L persistent behaviour or integrations; XL shared systems/world changes), not days. **Keep + XS means no implementation**, only optional verification. Dual costs separate a useful cheap slice from a fuller donor adaptation. Impact = Low / Medium / High; ROI = High / Medium / Low / Uncertain. A row's judgement is a discussion proposal, not approval.
