## Grouping and overlap guardrails

- **Lost-treasure chronicles:** the elder's winter favour, axe dispute, encoded-chronicle search and deciphering may form one connected story arc without requiring every minor favour. The cemetery-favour record is absorbed into Q14's existing cemetery-care incident; lawfully obtaining permission to inspect a grave may still be relevant to the chronicle search. Respect D-QUEST-29's lawful/unauthorised grave distinction.
- **Hunters:** both brotherhood records belong to a potential professional story arc, but remain separate episodes. V2 G07 can share hunter NPC context without being the same event.
- **Mysteries/people/property:** the four investigations are distinct incidents. Shared cave or missing-person motifs do not establish duplicate world events.
- **Treasure:** four separate V1 premises are retained provisionally; V2 Q05, Q11, Q12 and Q13 are comparison candidates, not automatic merge targets. Any actual overlap in unique location, ownership or incident must be reconciled during detailed curation.
- V1 `drewno-dla-anny` and `drewno-dla-piotra` are **not** part of this new batch: already decided under D-QUEST-22.

## Deferred for detailed design

The author explicitly **did not accept legacy reward amounts, reputation scores, artificial quest timers, role assignments, cave availability, mandatory combat, new mechanics or guaranteed implementation**. At M7, perform source/world-contract recon and decide minimum viable stages, alternative solutions, economy/reward nerfs, NPC identity, consequences, and whether a proposed story must wait for common systems. Use persistent world state, real item transfers, evidence-driven exposure, and canonical decisions AD-41…AD-58; no quest-only parallel subsystems.
