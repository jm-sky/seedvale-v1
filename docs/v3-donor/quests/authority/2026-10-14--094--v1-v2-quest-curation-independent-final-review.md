### H01 — HIGH / CONFIRMED gap, prospective collision: no SeedVale incident-ownership arbitration

**Evidence / canon:** [D-QUEST-16](../decisions/wolf-quest-curation.md), [D-QUEST-8/14/15](../decisions/DECISIONS.md), [review 024 §3](2026-10-09--024--quests-v1-v2-consistency-review.md), [generated families](../quests/catalog/v1/_generated/README.md), [quest engine port row](../state/PORT-LEDGER.md), [AD-42](2026-10-12--059--v1-v2-v3-content-gap-audit.md). ## Reward, exploration and portfolio-quality assessment

**Strengths:** fast low-friction errands (Mark's water, Kate's shells, Ann's message, single fox), social/ethical choice (G02, G06, G08, cemetery access, Q07, elder axe dispute), combat danger (boars, wolves, Q12 bear), interpretation/investigation (chronicle, ruins, shipwreck, tower) and several substantial optional treasure arcs. Quest NPCs act for their own reasons; quiet help and consequences while the player does nothing are explicitly accepted.

**Gaps to correct without rewriting canon:** (1) early treasure/gear opportunities need actual reachable M3/M7 placement and play-test proof; (2) too many attractive stories currently end in complex M8–M10 dependencies, so a first playable M7 sample needs deliberate short/medium/long selection rather than 51 full rewrites; (3) early equipment economy must accommodate rewarding finds and meaningful purchases, not require an unfinished production market; (4) no tested time-to-reward, material liquidity, unique item persistence or progression coverage yet.

**Do not nerf automatically.** Legacy V1 wolf sword/horse figures and old `renown +95` stack were a previous *same-den credit/claim* issue, not evidence that excellent swords are intrinsically too generous. One extraordinary blade after a significant adventure (Q12) is welcome. E01's existing legendary sword is **owned property**, not a generic loot roll or automatic chronicle payout (D-NPC-23). Author-backed reward exceptions (AD-48) can be justified, but coin transfers should use existing owners/funding or an explicit authored reason. Judge inappropriate progression jumps by tested alternative content, difficulty, source and availability, not by price alone.
