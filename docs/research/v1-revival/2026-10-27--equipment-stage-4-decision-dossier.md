## Author-independent technical recommendations (not gameplay approval)

- Keep the whole donor catalogue + all fields unchanged; Stage 3 canonical manifest, validation, fail-closed adapter and explicit source/target references remain authoritative for *source-to-V1 policy*, **not** items, combat or availability. Once approvals land, update compatibility policy with independent regression tests, never loosen validation or make name-based aliases global.
- New `ItemKind` entries need paired `ITEM_DEFS` and `ITEM_CATALOG` plus existing instance classification, `HeldTool`/primary choices, player/NPC combat or worn resolution, visual fallback, exact-ID transfers and save round-trips. Do not materialize donor `durability` or `sharpness` as mutable V1 fields; V1 melee normalization/quality and restriction formulas remain primary.
- For **melee**, explicitly author `damage`, `range`, `arcDot`, `windUp`, `hitWindow`, `recovery`, `staminaCost`; also defense policy, maintenance kind, held model, tool capabilities and NPC family ranking review. No V3 cooldown/reach conversion. **Ranged** needs `accuracy`, criticals, `projectileSpeed`, draw/recovery/stamina, `ammoKinds` priority and optional true `reloadTime`; no implicit crossbow reload or donor range replacement.
- **Armor** needs scalar `damageReduction`, restrictions (neutral defaults explicit), mass, quality handling and exact slot, applied *once* after active defense, with NPC parity and non-stacking under policy. No V3 typed resists or layered global mitigation. Six old slots are the actual runtime model until 044 lands.
- **Ammo** uses count-backed existing V1 selection/consumption and projectile recovery. New ammo variants require per-shooter compatibility and bonus/priority design; no new behavior implied by `damageType`. Existing `arrow`, `broadhead_arrow`, `war_arrow`, `bolt` and `stone` counts/balances stay unchanged.
- UI: keep selection-only rows, details-owned actions, derived comparison and exactly owned equipment/held state in current three-panel/hybrid implementation. Never display unimplemented typed resistance, DPS, parry or layering as an active stat.
- Patches may not go to runtime during performance-first freeze. Planning, tests, inert documentation/source-only metadata are permitted when they do not affect benchmark workload. Do not demand GPU/world benchmarks for isolated catalogue or save tests. No new V3 herbs, tea, food, raw materials, recipes or other goods assessment.
