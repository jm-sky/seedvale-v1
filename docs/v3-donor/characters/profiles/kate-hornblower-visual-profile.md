## Character and face

Kate is a **34-year-old merchant from Saltmere**. Naturally attractive, warm, friendly and self-assured, she has her own taste and understated class without appearing to be an aristocrat or a wealthy merchant.

Her face is realistic and feminine, with soft features, delicate freckles and a natural complexion. She has kind, intelligent eyes and a subtle, slightly mischievous smile. She should look like a mature woman with lived experience, **not an overly idealized beauty**. Her approachable, open energy matters as much as the costume.

## Hair — color and two hairstyles

- **Canonical color:** warm **copper auburn brown** — distinctly reddish but natural, **not vivid orange**. Hair is thick and slightly wavy, with loose strands framing her face.
- **Braid:** long, relaxed, slightly informal, worn over a shoulder or down her back.
- **Bun:** practical, loosely gathered and slightly messy, inspired by Kate's Seedvale v1 design.

Both hairstyles are valid for the same character and should preserve her recognisable face, hair color and silhouette.

## Clothing — selected variant 3

A stylish **medieval merchant dress in muted, deep green**. A cream linen underlayer, modest neckline, fitted bodice and naturally draping skirt give the costume a grounded, practical silhouette. Its finishing is more careful than that of an ordinary settlement resident, but without excessive wealth or courtly fashion.

Subtle inspiration from Quaternius **Wizard/Noble** models is permitted *only in tailoring details*: decorative trim, contrasting edging, elegant fabric layers and delicately structured shoulders. **No magic, armor or extravagant ornamentation.**

**Secondary palette:** mustard-gold scarf or shawl, cream linen and brown leather. Modest adornment should underline her occupation and taste rather than social rank.

## Accessories and trade

A good leather belt, coin pouch, small merchant shoulder bag, optionally keys or a trade notebook/ledger, and sturdy leather boots. Details should communicate commerce in **tools, weapons, herbs and rare objects** rather than a vegetable stall.

Her clothing and accessories are carefully maintained, practical for a working trader and still feminine. She is neither a rough peasant in generic work clothes nor a rich noblewoman.

## Overall presence and consistency

Kate is confident, socially warm, perceptive and experienced. Her appearance should make sense alongside the rougher and more austere Mark Hornblower while remaining visibly distinct: more refined and colorful, yet equally grounded in daily Saltmere life. Do not turn this contrast into a wealth or intelligence hierarchy.

**Reference selection:** the author's **variant 3** — deep green dress, mustard-gold scarf, merchant accessories and restrained Wizard/Noble tailoring cues. **The deliberate change from the selected variant is a clearly warmer copper-auburn-brown hair color.** Other design elements above remain the reference direction.
