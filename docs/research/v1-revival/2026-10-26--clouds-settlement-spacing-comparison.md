## 1. Executive findings

**Clouds:** V3 is a useful, active donor for a global directional cloud layer, but selection still needs one bounded V1 visual/performance check. Its clouds are two scrolling noise samples inside the existing sky-dome shader, with no additional cloud draw. That supports a submission-cost hypothesis, **not a measured V1 speedup**: the shader shades the sky on every frame and has different visual capabilities. Adapt the cloud technique to V1's rendering boundary; copying the whole V3 atmosphere would also replace sky colouring, sun presentation, exposure and fog policy, exceeding this research direction.

**Journeys:** V1's narrow primary adjustment boundary is `SETTLEMENT_GRID_STEP = 280`, shared by placement, coordinate lookup and adjacent-cell relationships. This is a cell pitch, **not an enforced minimum distance or a road-length target**. Modestly increasing it moves non-home candidates over unchanged terrain, which can change acceptance, settlement sizes, neighbours, crossings and content bindings. No exact multiplier is justified without seeded route and journey comparisons. Preserve movement speeds, time scale, settlement footprints and accepted fog/rendering distance as controls.

**Important qualification to author expectation:** landmarks and fauna have mixed distribution owners. More road length can expose more existing content, but neither unchanged density nor more danger/encounters follows automatically. In particular, the inspected ordinary wild-fauna construction is centred on home, not a uniform population per square metre.

Recommendations below are research alternatives and verification inputs for Opus, **not approved implementation scope, a work sequence or a generator redesign**.

### Cost, resources and lifecycle

**Source-derived work, not measurements:** V1 updates 28 transforms/visibility flags and four material tints/opacities per frame. Shared materials do not batch Sprites into one draw; there are up to 28 eligible cloud sprite submissions before view/visibility culling, plus the existing sky, with alpha-overlap cost. This is not an observed renderer draw count. Normal updates allocate small appearance/weight objects and temporary array literals; recycling reuses sprites. Four textures use the shared URL loader; actual dimensions/resident GPU bytes were not measured.

V3 has one sky draw per scene render and two texture samples per upper-sky fragment, including low/zero cover because the branch checks direction, not cover. It generates a deterministic 256×256 RGBA noise texture once (262,144 bytes base data; GPU allocation/mipmap overhead additional), mipmaps/repeat/linear filters, one shader material and sphere. Tick/follow reuse uniforms/vectors; no sprite loop or asynchronous texture fetch. Pixel count, shader arithmetic and how often the scene is rendered still matter: “no extra draw” does not mean zero GPU cost. A separate overlay over V1 Sky would add a pass and would **not** inherit the donor's no-extra-draw property.

V1 clouds live at app lifetime beside ground fog/particles, outside WorldBundle. App disposal removes the cloud group and disposes owned materials; [loadTexture](https://github.com/jm-sky/seedvale-v1/blob/6108c0b6fa0d2899e35187fe1a6e9fb4e48851f0/src/assets/loadTexture.ts) shares textures, so the cloud system must not dispose them. Its pending load continuation has no disposed guard in the inspected function: late completion is a lifecycle exposure to cover when adapting, not a reproduced leak. V3 disposes sphere geometry/material, but SkyDome.dispose does not explicitly dispose its owned noise texture. Define ownership/cleanup for that texture in an adaptation; material disposal alone is not explicit texture disposal.

**Narrow seam:** reusable donor pieces are noise generation, virtual-plane sampling, horizon fade, cover smoothing and offset accumulation. Feed them with V1 weather-derived appearance/coverage and V1 sun/day-night presentation, preserving current weather, sky/fog ownership, debug toggle, water/post-processing interactions and app teardown. Opus decides shader composition. Do not simply re-enable Three Sky's existing cloud branch: V1 source documents prior horizon precision rectangles and excess sun/bloom brightness there.

**Capabilities at risk:** the four PNG silhouettes, distinct light/dense height/scale/speed families, slow population turnover, seasonal family bias and finite-position perspective. V3 instead supplies a continuous procedural deck and cloud/sun masking. Whether that trade preserves V1 identity is unverified; existing ground fog, precipitation, lightning and accepted visibility distances remain preservation inputs, not replacements.

## 4. V1 spacing controls and dependency map

Primary owners: [settlementGenerator](https://github.com/jm-sky/seedvale-v1/blob/6108c0b6fa0d2899e35187fe1a6e9fb4e48851f0/src/settlement/settlementGenerator.ts), [findSettlementSite](https://github.com/jm-sky/seedvale-v1/blob/6108c0b6fa0d2899e35187fe1a6e9fb4e48851f0/src/settlement/findSettlementSite.ts), [families/size weights](https://github.com/jm-sky/seedvale-v1/blob/6108c0b6fa0d2899e35187fe1a6e9fb4e48851f0/src/settlement/families.ts), [progression policy](https://github.com/jm-sky/seedvale-v1/blob/6108c0b6fa0d2899e35187fe1a6e9fb4e48851f0/src/settlement/settlementProgression.ts), [world defaults](https://github.com/jm-sky/seedvale-v1/blob/6108c0b6fa0d2899e35187fe1a6e9fb4e48851f0/src/config/worldConfig.ts), [shared definition cache](https://github.com/jm-sky/seedvale-v1/blob/6108c0b6fa0d2899e35187fe1a6e9fb4e48851f0/src/settlement/settlementPlanCache.ts).

- Cell pitch 280; worldToCell rounds X/Z divided by pitch; IDs are `gx_gz`. Non-home centre jitter is ±15% of pitch (currently ±42) per axis, using deterministic cell seed. The generator does not perform a pairwise settlement-distance rejection pass; geometric separation comes from grid/jitter/site-search bounds.
- Home is always cell(0,0), centred initially at origin; its seed is the world seed. Ordinary site search samples 80 candidates within ±24, rejects wet plaza, active river footprint, local height delta >2.2 and footprint dry ratio <0.4, then scores terrain/resource attraction. Non-home failure returns null; home retries ±72/±120, finally falls back to its centre.
- Size is rolled from terrain at candidate-cell centre **before** site search, then locked except resource-outpost override. SM/MD/LG/XL have footprint radii40/48/56/72 and family ranges1–3/3–5/5–7/7–9; OUTPOST radius22/family1. Home reserves two families and auto clamps to at most MD. Terrain weights differ substantially, so retaining cell RNG does not retain size if the moved centre crosses a biome.
- Auto-home progression selects one feasible near-ring cell (Chebyshev1–2) with a larger minimum and one far-ring cell(3–5) at least LG. Explicit home-size override disables these rings. Larger pitch stretches their physical reach and can change which candidates qualify.
- The inspected placement/lookup model uses signed cells and regional terrain samplers, without a V3-style fixed continent-edge clamp. Region scales (continent2200, mountain1800, moisture2000; Worley400) are independent world-space controls. Streaming/render rings are not world boundaries and must not be enlarged for this goal.

[Persistence ownership](https://github.com/jm-sky/seedvale-v1/blob/6108c0b6fa0d2899e35187fe1a6e9fb4e48851f0/docs/state/persistence.md) distinguishes regenerated world definitions from durable deltas.
