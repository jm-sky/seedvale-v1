## Approved narrative — 2026-10-14

An ordinary, pre-planned family supper at Ann and Peter Woodward's home, independent of repairs, emergencies or completion of another quest. Mark and Kate Hornblower and the player are invited when the player has an established friendly relationship with the household. This is a small social occasion, not a manufactured crisis.

Preparations draw on real household stores, food freshness, firewood and cooking capacity. Ann handles the meal; Peter has regular woodcutting work, while Mark must cover his evening watch. The player can contribute food or kitchen work, help Peter finish early, or cover a bounded part of Mark's rounds using normal torch/post state. These are alternative contributions rather than all mandatory gates.

Three valid meal arrangements:
- **One table:** household members and guests eat together if practical duties and cooking are covered; stronger personal relationship gains and an NPC memory of the occasion.
- **Two sittings:** duties continue and all are fed in shifts; moderate relationship gains and real fuel/food use.
- **At the doorway:** Mark can keep watch, neighbours can share the hospitality; smaller household relationship benefit, modest local reputation recognition.

If the player declines, leaves, or cannot complete preparation, the NPCs continue the supper themselves; no artificial player penalty. Real interruptions such as an actual threat take priority, and the group adjusts its arrangement instead of freezing Mark's duty. Rewards are a real meal, personal goodwill and at most a small household gift from available supplies; no automatic coin payout. Food, fuel, NPC needs, relationships and settlement reputation use their shared systems rather than quest-only inventory or social effects.

No additional Woodward family members are invented merely to reproduce V2's Hewer cast. G03 remains a distinct investigation. The accepted D-QUEST-13 scheduling rule applies if Mark is temporarily occupied: the relevant G03 dusk scene can happen on a subsequent dusk. Exact dialogues, numerical rewards, guest availability and interaction timings belong to M7.
