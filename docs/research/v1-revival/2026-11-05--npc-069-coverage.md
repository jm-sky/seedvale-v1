| quest_accepted | src/ui-vue/store.ts:acceptNpcDialogueOffer after onAccept. Acknowledges willingness, not task completion or guaranteed success. |
| quest_declined | src/ui-vue/store.ts:closeNpcDialogueMenu with pending helpResult.offer. Decline, not generic farewell. |
| quest_complete | src/quests/QuestManager.ts:playQuestCompleteSound via complete outcome, loaded giver resolved in createApp. Generic thanks only; no victory, restored animal, reward or completed repair claim. |
