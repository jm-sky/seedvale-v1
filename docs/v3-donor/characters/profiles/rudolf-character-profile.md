# Rudolf — character profile

**Status:** author-approved personality direction; surname pending; editorial reference, not runtime implementation  
**Created at:** 2026-10-29  
**Domain:** authored NPC character, dialogue, quests  
**Identity:** Rudolf, reeve (Sołtys) of the home settlement; surname and stable actor binding to be reconciled with V1

## Creative anchor

Rudolf is a composed, sensible and experienced village leader. He is somewhat wealthier than most residents and quietly proud of his position and accomplishments. He appreciates being treated with respect and can occasionally seem slightly superior, but is generally fair, decent and approachable. He genuinely cares about the settlement and its people.

## Personality and contradictions

- Practical, steady and capable of making balanced decisions.
- Takes justified pride in his household, prosperity and role as reeve.
- Sometimes mildly condescending or status-conscious, never a caricature of an arrogant noble.
- Usually benevolent and fair; keeps the community's well-being in mind.
- Appreciates courtesy and earned trust, without turning every interaction into a demand for deference.

## Dialogue and quest direction

His manner is measured, confident and a little formal, but not theatrical or pompous. A subtle hint of pride should appear only occasionally. Avoid portraying him as corrupt, cruel or obsessed with wealth.

Quest connections: settlement matters, disputes, reputation and the legitimate authorization to excavate the **specific home-cemetery grave** associated with the bear-cave treasure map. Kazimierz may recommend the player; the recommendation waives an up-front fee but **not** the settlement's agreed share of treasure actually retained. Payments belong to the settlement treasury, not Rudolf personally.

This profile does **not** implement permission rules, add quests, set dialogue lines or override existing quest and simulation state.
