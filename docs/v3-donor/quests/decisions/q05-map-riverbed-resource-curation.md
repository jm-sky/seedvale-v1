# Q05 — The Map That Missed the River: Curation

**Created at:** 2026-10-14  
**Created by:** ChatGPT Luna

**Status:** Accepted narrative direction (author, 2026-10-14); detailed design deferred.

## Decision — keep / rewrite

Keep and rewrite V2 Q05 as Rosalind Marchant's family-treasure investigation. The old map's missing river bend leads to a plausible dry former channel and a stone-circle treasure site. Do not merge with V1 `mapa-do-skarbu`: its dark-forest ruins and treasure are a separate incident, subject to their own future curation.

The dry riverbed is primarily a geographical clue for finding the treasure. It may also be a useful route, but do **not** promise or create a new trade route or seasonal caravan path solely for Q05. Whether a shortcut exists and can be used must follow canonical geography, travel and world-simulation contracts. Retain the possibility of sharing route knowledge with Percy or local travellers where supported; separate that choice from disposition of the treasure.

Rosalind's ownership and the player's payment (money or a valuable gem) are distinct from sharing knowledge of the route. Keep legitimate return/negotiation and possible appropriation, with persistent item ownership, real transfers and evidence-based exposure; no omniscient punishment. Exact rewards, NPC follow-through and interactions remain for M7/M8 and applicable shared systems.

## Optional resource discovery — author direction

If canonical geography and resource generation plausibly place a deposit in or around the dry channel, the player may discover it as **optional exploration**, never a required Q05 objective. Information about the resource may be passed to local craftspeople; possible future economic effects require shared knowledge, professions and economy, not a quest-only production mechanic. Discovery does not automatically confer exclusive ownership or prevent the player from later participating in extraction under general world rules. A player outpost and sale of extracted resources are possible future sandbox gameplay, **not an approved Q05 feature or a promise of outpost support**.

**Author preference (not a resource-type decision):** The author welcomes clay as a world resource and clay-item production such as pottery. The optional Q05 deposit is not committed to clay: another resource, potentially valuable gold, may be more appropriate if the geological/world-generation design supports it. Neither clay, gold, mining, pottery nor a deposit at this location is guaranteed by this decision.

## Scope and unresolved implementation contracts

- Reuse M3 geographical discoveries, world resources, NPC knowledge, ordinary ownership and economy; never generate a deposit only when Q05 activates.
- No bespoke mining, pottery, caravan routing, settlement economy or outpost subsystem for this quest.
- The existence and location of the old channel, site and deposit must be verified against eventual generated geography. If unsuitable, adjust or defer the optional beat rather than force world generation.
- Detailed plot branching, calibration, resource identity, rights of extraction and NPC response remain open for their relevant shared-system design gates.
- Imported V2 stages, timers, rewards and technical proposals are reference material, not automatically accepted canon.
