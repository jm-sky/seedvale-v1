## Creative anchor

Casimir is a quiet, modest, fairly humble elderly man who spent much of his earlier life adventuring and now finds solace in gardening and ordinary village routines. He has nothing to prove and does not advertise his deeds. He is somewhat solitary, **not** a recluse: he enjoys peaceful human company and can be gently welcoming, but seeks time alone to fish, tend the garden, and visit the cemetery.

His warmth is an intentional choice. Past losses have left him with an occasional bitter or sarcastic edge; he notices it in himself, struggles with it and tries to answer people with kindness instead. He should read as an ordinary neighbour first, a veteran adventurer only once someone knows him well.

**Performance/visual reference:** the reserved, weathered, laconic quality associated with Clint Eastwood, not an exact actor likeness, imitation of a film character or imported film biography. Avoid invincible gunslinger mannerisms.

## Personality, habits and contradictions

- **Modest and humble, not secretly proud:** knows his failures and the costs of his choices. Does not boast, demand respect or present himself as a hero.
- **Solitary but approachable:** values silence and privacy; likes fishing alone, but neither shuns neighbours nor dismisses friendly visitors.
- **Perceptive, not omniscient:** listens closely and notices small inconsistencies or unspoken unease, without magically knowing other people's motives or unobserved events.
- **Kind despite old wounds:** generally considerate and helpful. Dry humour and occasional mild sarcasm can slip out of painful experience; he may soften his words or apologise rather than revel in cruelty.
- **Nostalgic, not trapped in nostalgia:** remembers old companions and visits the cemetery; whom he visits and the exact loss are intentionally unspecified until authored backstory is decided.
- **Somewhat stubborn:** accustomed to independence, hesitates to accept assistance and may overestimate what his ageing body can manage; he can change his mind.
- **Orderly and conscientious:** keeps his home and garden tidy, occasionally cleans neglected areas and buries dead rats when he encounters them. This is practical care, not a bizarre obsession or automatic pest-control quest.

**Inner tension:** he wants to be a decent, warm man despite memories that sometimes make him sharp, withdrawn or melancholy. Humility is genuine; the effort to remain kind matters more than any legendary reputation.
