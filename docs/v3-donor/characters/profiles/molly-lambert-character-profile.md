# Molly Lambert — character profile

**Status:** author-approved name and personality direction; editorial reference, not runtime implementation  
**Created at:** 2026-10-29  
**Domain:** authored NPC character, dialogue, quests  
**Identity:** Molly Lambert, shepherdess of the home settlement; stable V1 NPC binding pending

## Creative anchor

Molly is a gentle, cheerful and dreamy shepherdess. She loves nature, animals and quiet hours in the meadows. She sometimes drifts into thought, imagining distant places and stories beyond the settlement. Her warmth and romantic outlook make her distinct from the more practical and duty-driven residents.

## Personality

- Kind, empathetic and inclined to see good in people, even after they have made mistakes.
- Cheerful and affectionate toward her flock; she knows the animals as individuals.
- Dreamy and sometimes absent-minded, but not incapable or indifferent to her work.
- Trusting without being entirely oblivious to danger or wrongdoing.
- Prefers understanding and reconciliation over revenge, while caring deeply about the safety of those in her charge.

## Dialogue and visual direction

Her language should feel warm, natural, imaginative and gently expressive, with occasional observations about the meadows, animals or imagined journeys. Avoid making her childish, helpless or relentlessly sentimental. Portrait expressions can show thoughtful calm, open affection, concern for Pip and quiet relief or hope.

## Quest connections

**Lost Lamb (G01/R36):** Molly owns Pip, a real persistent young sheep taken before the player's arrival by Piers, a hungry traveller. Pip's loss genuinely distresses her; her first priority is the lamb's safe return rather than punishing the thief. If Piers admits the theft and agrees to restitution, she is willing to forgive him and accept him into the household as a resident and worker, subject to the approved real-world housing, food and continuity requirements.

The player may resolve the theft through evidence-based confrontation, real payment, discreet retrieval or guard Mark's lawful intervention. None of these is automatically a guaranteed successful recovery; actual animal, NPC and inventory state remains authoritative. Reconciliation and camp dismantling must remain physical and persistent rather than dialogue-only outcomes.

This profile does not add quests, spawn Pip or Piers, guarantee a shepherd through the current V1 staffing system, or change runtime NPC identities.

## Naming and integration notes

The author selected **Molly Lambert**, replacing the earlier proposed first name Helena. The V3 donor Q0 cast already uses Molly Lambert for a shepherd, but its household and spouse details are not automatically adopted as V1 runtime canon. The home-settlement shepherd identity and family composition need explicit reconciliation with V1 generation and quest bindings.
