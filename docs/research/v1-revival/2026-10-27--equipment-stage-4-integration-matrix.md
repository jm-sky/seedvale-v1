## Reading and invariant contracts

Every V3 canonical ID is present exactly once. The **proposed V1 runtime ID** is either an existing identity, new distinct proposed kind, or **reserved** for a source-only future item; `reserved` means *not registered*. Existing aliases mean an explicitly reviewed **source reference**, not an automatic reclassification of V1 saves or equipment. Full V3 definitions, including availability, layer, material, damage types, quality, tier, grip, ranges, wear and parry fields, stay lossless and inert where unsupported.

**Functional support** is *proposed*, not implemented. Existing-V1 items keep actual V1 numbers/behavior; `new` rows require authored profiles even when family mechanics already exist. Under-layer inventory-only and the nine unsupported future definitions are deliberately named limitations, not blanket deferral. Merchant/loot/reward suggestions require finite/source-specific reviews; **no item is made globally available because V3 says active**. `unique` and `quest-only` rows have no enabled source before an explicitly keyed issuance contract.

Required authored V1 profiles for each new kind (column abbreviations):
- **M-`subclass`:** tune exact V1 melee `damage/range/arcDot/windUp/hitWindow/recovery/staminaCost`; defense eligibility, agility/recovery, maintenance durability/edge, grip/held visual, NPC classification, item capacities and trade price. Source reach/cooldown/damage must **not** be copied.
- **R-`subclass`:** tune V1 `damage/range/projectileSpeed/drawTime/recovery/staminaCost/accuracy/criticalChance/criticalMultiplier`, ordered ammo kinds, NPC/player shot behavior, optional crossbow reload, held model, value/instance. Source donor units/numbers are non-authoritative.
- **A-`slot/layer/family`:** tune V1 `damageReduction` scalar and restriction multipliers, weight/size, V1 quality and avatar presentation. For `torso` use `body`; `shoulders` and `forearms` require plan `items-player-044`. No typed or location-based resist, no accidental second multiplier. **I** under-layer candidates have ownership/visual/quantity only, no equip/reduction.
- **H-`shieldClass`:** proposed held-only `DefenseConfig`, no offhand or donor timed parry; blocked until A7. Requires held choice, accurate UI and save/visual identity; `buckler` is V3-future.
- **Q-`ammoKind`:** count-backed `ItemKind`, shooter compatibility/selection order, damage-bonus authored in V1 scale, pickup/drop/consumption and NPC ammo owner. `arrow`/ `stone` retain V1 balancing instead.

**Persistence shorthand:** new stable instance IDs require `createAcquiredInstance`, `cloneItemInstance`, `toSaveItemInstance`, `Inventory.instancesFromJSON`, drops/transfers and structural load validation; count-based ammo needs count/save/recovery; new armor includes quality+equipment IDs, new held defensive items include held IDs, unique issuances need stable consumed/reward state. A source-only reserved definition creates **no** save entry. Schema/version changes, if needed, must coordinate with the Strategic Brief's versioned-save direction.
