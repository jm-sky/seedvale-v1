# Archibald — character profile

**Status:** author-approved personality direction; editorial reference, not runtime implementation  
**Domain:** authored NPC, personality, dialogue, relationships, quests  
**Identity:** Archibald, 74-year-old elder of a neighbouring settlement; `characterKey: archibald`; surname procedurally generated

## Creative anchor

Archibald is a proud, sociable, talkative old man with a sharp wit, a good heart and a long memory for promises and grievances. He enjoys company, colourful stories and lively arguments. Though generally fair and helpful, he has trouble admitting that he might be wrong.

**Central contradiction:** Archibald believes deeply in honesty and keeping one's word, but his devotion to being treated fairly can make him stubborn and unforgiving.

## Past — travelling merchant

**Proposed backstory:** Archibald spent decades travelling between settlements, trading everyday goods such as tools, grain and hides. He was neither wealthy nor famous; he made his living through bargaining, persistence and relationships. Over time he learned local histories, routes, rumours and the reputations of people he dealt with. Now retired from travelling, he lives modestly and tends his household.

His merchant past makes him a plausible source of introductions and local knowledge, **not** an archaeologist or an all-knowing narrator. He likes to embellish anecdotes to entertain listeners; genuine facts must remain distinct from his exaggerations.

## Personality and ordinary life

- **Proud and independent:** resents being treated as helpless and values what he earned through his own work.
- **Gregarious and curious:** welcomes travellers, trades news with neighbours and enjoys a spirited debate.
- **Stubborn but capable of growth:** holds grudges, yet can reconsider when challenged by someone he trusts.
- **Honest but not always fair:** prizes promises and debts, sometimes overlooking another person's reasonable perspective.
- **Warm beneath his grumbling:** helps those in need, even while complaining about the trouble.
- **Playfully boastful:** has a quick, teasing sense of humour and sometimes improves a story for dramatic effect.

He might sit outside watching the settlement, visit neighbours, maintain his small household and offer unsolicited advice. These are characterization ideas, not guaranteed schedules or newly implemented activities.

## Dialogue voice

Lively, direct and expressive, with teasing remarks and occasional long anecdotes. In serious conversations he becomes sincere, even if admitting fault costs him pride.

**Illustrative English lines — not approved runtime dialogue:**

- Greeting a stranger: “A new face! Good. Perhaps I'll finally hear a story I haven't heard a dozen times already.”
- On the missing axe: “It's not about the axe! I could buy another tomorrow. It's about a man's word.”
- To a trusted player: “For someone half my age, you have an irritating habit of making sense.”
- After reconciliation: “Fine. Perhaps I've carried this long enough. Don't tell everyone I admitted it.”

## Story connections and boundaries

In **Stara uraza**, Archibald disputes whether a neighbour returned his borrowed axe. Neither version is established as unquestionably true. The player can take his side or encourage reconciliation, with trust affecting his response. The dispute opens the archaeological thread.

In **Zakodowana kronika**, Archibald introduces the player to an archaeologist and may provide a stronger lead based on relationships and reputation. **Porządek na cmentarzu** belongs to the cemetery caretaker, not Archibald.

Archibald must remain distinct from home elder **Kazimierz Nowak**: Kazimierz is a quiet, humble former adventurer seeking peace; Archibald is an outspoken former merchant who still struggles to let old grievances go.
