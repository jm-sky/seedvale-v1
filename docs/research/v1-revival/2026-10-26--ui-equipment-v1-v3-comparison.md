## Risks of losing V1 capabilities

- A polished inventory could silently lose exact instance/quality selection, separate primary weapons, physical drops, liquid content/species/freshness information, capacity/size constraints and contextual item use.
- A character reskin could hide effective/base attributes, earned XP, active conditions, all five gear restrictions, settlement-specific reputation/renown/badges or actionable skill targeting.
- A conversation transplant could remove trade with ordinary NPCs, artisan orders/gifts/wages, report-before-reward, multi-quest routing, area research or joining; pausing/holding the NPC differently could change needs and hazards.
- A single-use prompt could replace the broader Quick Actions categories, placement/cancellation and disabled reasons. A second modal/key owner could leave held inputs stuck or dispatch commands into a rebuilt world.
- Equipping by kind/count or importing donor IDs could detach the piece from trade, corpse loot, quests and Save. New armor must not mint duplicate gear on NPC reconstruction.
