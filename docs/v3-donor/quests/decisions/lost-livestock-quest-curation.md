# Lost Livestock Quest Curation

**Created at:** 2026-10-10  
**Created by:** ChatGPT Luna

- Lost livestock may belong to any household, regardless of the owner's profession.
- A loss may be caused by an event such as a storm or wolves.
- v1 `zagubiona-owca` is an instance of the general lost-livestock quest mechanism, not authored content that must be preserved.
- Preserve v2 G01 `The Lost Lamb` as an authored quest: Molly Lambert's story about her stolen lamb Pip.
- Generated lost-livestock quests represent separate, actual lost-animal incidents. **Two or more simultaneous quests may concern different individual animals**, even in one settlement, with the same species and similar loss circumstances. **The same animal may be lost and recovered and then lost again**: each genuine occurrence has a new persistent incident identity. If an authored and generated offer point to **the same animal and same concrete loss/theft occurrence**, do not invent two independent physical disappearances, incompatible rescue claims or duplicate world outcomes; compatible notices, objectives and separate justified one-time rewards can coexist ([D-QUEST-16](quest-incident-arbitration-contract.md)).

## Related animal quests — author decision (2026-10-10)

- The injured-cow quest is a separate quest, not an extension or duplicate of the lost-livestock incident.
- The injured, hungry dog is a separate authored quest. Its intended outcome is that the dog becomes the player's companion.
- Do not merge these quests merely because they concern animals. The general lost-livestock mechanism must not create a competing task for the same animal and same incident as an authored quest.
- This is a curation/design decision only; quest-system implementation remains gated by M7.
