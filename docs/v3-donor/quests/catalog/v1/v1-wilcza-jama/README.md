## Summary

The wolves come from a nearby den. Anna asks the player to find it and clear it. The quest needs Anna's relation at trusted and the `grozny-wilk` quest reported first. Completion is when the den holds no wolves.

## Stages / objectives

- Clear the wolf den (`clear_wolf_den`, `WOLF_DEN_ID`).

## Outcomes & rewards

- `reported` (complete, reward shown): an obsidian sword, relation +3 with Anna, reputation competence +15, courage +18, benevolence +6, and renown +25.
