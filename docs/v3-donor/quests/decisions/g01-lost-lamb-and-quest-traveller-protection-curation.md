## D-QUEST-42 — G01

Keep/rewrite V2 G01 `Lost Lamb` as the authored theft of Molly Lambert's lamb Pip by hungry travelling Piers. The generic lost-livestock incidents remain distinct; never generate a competing lost-livestock claim for the same Pip incident (see [lost livestock curation](lost-livestock-quest-curation.md)).

Retain four credible recovery routes: evidence-based confrontation, paying Piers, discreetly recovering Pip, or involving guard Mark Hornblower. Restitution of the lamb, real item/coin transfers, knowledge and reputation consequences must reflect the actual route. Avoid automatic punishment without credible detection or information propagation, and do not create bespoke human-combat or law mechanics.

**Accepted:** A peaceful resolution can let Piers make amends by working for Molly. Work is real NPC activity, with food and/or wages sourced from actual household resources, and can have persistent effects on their relationship and later encounters. It is not an automatic outcome of every peaceful option: Molly and Piers must plausibly agree. Use shared NPC work, needs, schedules, economy, relationship and travel systems; if those are not available at M7, stage/defer the follow-through rather than fake it as a quest-only labour system.

Piers and Pip are persistent world actors/property, not objects created and removed solely by a quest timer. Retire the imported arbitrary 48-hour disappearance: Piers may independently travel, sell the lamb or change plans, and the resulting ownership/whereabouts and recoverability persist. The player can miss an opportunity; consequences arise from events rather than a quest-only deadline. NPCs may also resolve the incident without the player. Exact timings, dialogue, amounts and runtime implementation remain deferred.

## Quest-linked NPC travel protection — accepted scope

**Only NPCs linked to quests** receive travel-survival bonuses during actual travel, not all travellers. Provide a strong **Sneak/stealth detection-avoidance boost** against wild animals and a temporary, danger-triggered **adrenaline boost** to stamina and escape speed. This is intended to make quest-linked travellers **difficult, not impossible, for wildlife to kill**. No invulnerability, teleport rescue, damage immunity, quest-only predator exemption, or permanent fighting advantage.

Bonuses apply **during travel only**, not throughout a quest association or ordinary combat. They should leverage the common NPC/fauna detection, movement, stamina and danger responses, not a parallel quest AI. The precise eligibility rule, stacking, cooldowns, magnitude and how travel phase is established require M4/M6 contract design and balancing. Preserve seeded/deterministic behaviour and persistence/recovery of injury or death; quests must accommodate the NPC's death and alternative/lapsed outcomes without resurrecting them.

This protection is a deliberate quest-linked exception to ordinary traveller balance, not a universal travel-safety rule.
